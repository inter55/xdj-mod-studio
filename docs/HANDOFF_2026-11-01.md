  (`0x10 .. 0x10+n*28`), not the bodies. A corrupted body therefore
  skips just its own payload while the others still arm — that is what

### 2026-11-02: stems replace the track on the stick (odd-chunk stall fixed)

Replacing a WAV/AIFF with its stems version hung the deck at loading, even
on **stock** firmware, so it was never our stems code. The size field in
export.pdb and the extra stems chunk were both ruled out by A/B playback;
the remaining structural difference was an **odd-length chunk**: RIFF/AIFF
put a pad byte after one, and the deck's parser loses its place on it.
ffmpeg's 115-byte AIFF ID3 chunk is the case that hit real tracks.

`tools/stems4.py` now rewrites the mix so **every chunk is even**: the zero
byte goes inside the chunk (and an ID3 chunk's own synchsafe size is bumped
too), so the chunk size, the tag size and the next header all agree. The
stems chunk body and its alignment padding are even as well. The app's stems
dialog no longer offers replacement as a choice -- a WAV/AIFF track is always
replaced under its own name (original kept as `.pre-stems`), so it stays in
ARTIST / TRACK with its cues.

Verified (`tools/stems4.py selftest`): WAV and AIFF round trips bit-exact,
chunks `fmt/LIST/data/xst4/xs4t` and `NAME/COMM/SSND/ID3/APPL/APPL` all even,
tags (artist/title/album) and duration still read by ffprobe, and a
synthetic odd chunk (7 B) comes out 8 B with the next chunk still found.
MP3/M4A still cannot be replaced (the deck picks the decoder from the file
extension), so those get a copy next to the original under BROWSE > FOLDER.
