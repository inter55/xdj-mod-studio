## 3. yupiyupijupjup (1.0.5, flashed, UTILITY 1.16): Touch FX -- sound goes, then the screen freezes

His words: happens with ALL effects, but only when swiping back and forth
fast or switching effects fast; first the sound disappears, then the screen
freezes; sometimes it comes back after a short wait, sometimes a restart is
needed; a "file error" came once.

Two causes fit, and they add up:

1. **Filter blow-up (fixed in 1.0.8).** A fast swipe crosses the corner
   where the 1.0.5 filter is unstable; from then on it feeds garbage into
   the deck's tempo engine. Switching effects passes through FILTER with
   the finger's last position, so "all effects" does not rule this out.
2. **Repaint storm (fixed 2026-11-10, not released).** Every visible change
   repainted the whole Touch FX panel, twice. One tap is three changes, a
   DEPTH drag is one per frame. On the real CPU that keeps the screen task
   busy for long stretches and the audio feed behind it waits: sound drops
   out first, then the screen hangs, and it recovers when the finger stops
   -- exactly his description, including for Crush and Gate, which have no
   filter in them. The panel now repaints at most once per 60 ms per layer.
   The emulator cannot show how slow a repaint is on the real chip, so this
   is a reasoned fix, to be confirmed on a deck.

## 4. Hot cues on 1.0.8: "A-C show up and trigger, but all cues, incl. A-C, are not present on the A-H bank" (2 pictures, not seen here)

**Cause found and reproduced (emulator, real firmware).** The module decided
"this pad has a cue" from one status value the deck stores per cue, and only
accepted the value 6. That is what the deck stores for the TEST cues the
module was developed with. Every hot cue in a real rekordbox export is
written slightly differently (status 0 in the file), and the deck then
stores 4. So on a real stick no cue ever counted as set: the A-H view drew
eight empty pads and D-H were never picked up from the track. The stock
A-C row kept working because the deck draws that itself. This also explains
the older "D-H empty on real exports" reports.

Proof: test stick rebuilt with cues written the way rekordbox writes them
(`HC_STATUS=0 tools/hotcue8_emu.py`): the released rule fails exactly like
the report ("cues seen: 8" FAIL, D/E/H pads dead); with the fix all hot cue
tests pass on that stick and on the two older ones. Not yet on a real deck.
`tools/hotcue_probe.py` (read-only) prints how a real deck stores a loaded
track's cues, to confirm it there.

## 5. Italian tester on 1.0.9 (cable): "stems work after 30 s; hot cues D-H shown but D..H always play A, B, C, gate on or off"

Hot cues: cause found and fixed (emulator-proven, not on a deck yet). The
mod wrote the cue's own number (4..8) into the deck's three pad slots; the
deck uses that number as a pad index, so a D-H press either replayed A-C or
stalled. Now the slots carry the pad number and the mod tells the deck to
reload the pad. All ten test presses land on their own cue in both
emulators. Gate Cue not separately tested.

Stems: 1.0.9 still ships the stems mute from 1.0, so ~30 s is expected
there. The faster mute is built for 1.1 but has not been heard on a deck.
